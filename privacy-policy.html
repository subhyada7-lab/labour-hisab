Labour Hisab Privacy Policy

This app is an offline-first attendance and payment record tool.

Data

Records in this version are stored locally on the device/browser. The app does not automatically upload them to a server.

Backup

Use Backup & Restore to export and restore your records. Keep backup files private.

Security

Protect your device, password and backup files. This package is a PWA; a production cloud login requires separate server authentication.
